# Assumptions – Aleksandra's simulated year (1 Jan – 31 Dec 2026)

Everything is regenerated by one command, reproducibly: `python src/generate_all.py` (options: `--seed`, `--lat/--lon/--city/--iata`,
`--offline`, `--stages`; run with `-h`). Default seed 2026 always gives byte-identical files; another seed gives another year.
Human variability (departure/wake/meal times, durations, appliance power, day-to-day mood) is normally distributed around habits,
truncated to sensible bounds. Rows before 29 Sep 2026 ("today", `--today`) are labelled `history`, later rows `future`.

## Pipeline (`src/generate_all.py`, code in `src/hackowatt/datagen/`)

| Stage | What it does | Output |
|---|---|---|
| weather | Downloads real Open-Meteo data for the city coordinates: **observed** (Historical Weather API) and the **forecasts as issued** (Historical Forecast API) for 2025 and replays them as 2026 (same month/day/hour). Observed weather drives the simulation, the issued forecast is what the forecasting model sees (mean abs. temperature error 1.07 °C). | `data/weather/` |
| calendar | Fully procedural, seeded .ics: seasonal anchors (holidays, Easter, All Saints, Christmas, ski week, summer holiday, two conferences, birthdays, service visits), Paul's visits and Berlin weekends every ~2 weeks, work trips (1 night, or 3–4 days; a few day trips) placed so that ~45 % of the year is trips; everyday office/WFH days, sport, social, chores, guests at home. | `data/calendar/2026-MM.ics` + `aleksandra_calendar_2026.ics` |
| itinerary | Parses the .ics (only input) into a gap-free timeline: at home, in transit, at a venue/hotel/airport; Paul at home; guests. Leave/return times get normal noise. | `data/geolocation/itinerary_truth.csv` |
| consumption | Minute-by-minute appliance simulation (physics below), aggregated to hourly kWh. | `data/consumption/` |
| geolocation | Phone position every 15 min sampled from the same timeline, with GPS-like noise (log-normal accuracy, ~1.5 % poor fixes). | `data/geolocation/positions.csv` |
| report | Checks every appliance against the scenario table and draws year/season/geolocation figures. | `data/consumption/validation.md`, `figures/` |

Outputs (unchanged formats): `hourly_consumption.csv` (observable: weather + kWh per appliance), `simulation_ground_truth.csv`
(not observable: validation only), `appliance_events.csv` (log with reasons), `positions.csv` (observable input for isHome).
Timestamps carry the real UTC offset (+01:00 / +02:00); the grid is 8 760 wall-clock hours, so the two DST days are simplified.

## Location

`--lat/--lon` are the city coordinates: they set weather, sunrise/sunset, home (0.8 km south-west of the point) and all local venues
(stored as offsets from the city centre, so they move with the city). Venue names in the calendar are Warsaw-flavoured; trip
destinations are real cities with fixed coordinates. Airports are ~12 km out of town, stations ~0.6 km.

## Persona rules (from Scenario 1, section 06/07)

- About 51 % of hours away from the flat (trips ~44 %, plus office days and evenings out); trips are irregular: one night, or 3–4 days for work, weekends with Paul in Berlin, family in Łódź, one ski week and one summer week.
- Wake 06:30–07:00 on weekdays (normal, σ 18 min), later at weekends; she wakes ~70 min before an early departure; bedtime ~23:00.
- Before/after trips: laundry (100 % when the calendar says "Laundry + pack", ~80 % after a trip), full dishwasher, comfort boost (+1 K for 2.5 h) after returning, groceries after long trips, power bank charging. Working from home adds daytime laptop/kettle/coffee use.
- She remembers holiday mode / the power strip on only 30 % of trips; ~4 % of departures leave lights on for 0.5–12 h.
- Seasons: heating on when the 3-day mean outdoor temperature is < 13 °C, off > 16 °C (frost protection 15 °C); colder mains water in winter means larger hot-water draws; more TV/tea in winter and on cloudy evenings; lighting follows daily sunrise/sunset and cloud cover; blinds halve solar gains above 24 °C; windows are aired when warm.

## Household & building

- 65 m² apartment in a well-insulated building. Heat loss 60 W/K, thermal capacity 8 MJ/K, effective solar window area 1.4 m².
- 90 % of appliance electricity ends up as heat inside the apartment. A person adds 80 W awake, 60 W asleep, and a guest 90 W.
- Windows are opened when it is above 24 °C inside and someone is awake (a tilted window at night above 23.5 °C). This stops the apartment overheating in early September.

## Heat pump (the "electric heating / heat pump" line from the list)

- **Space heating** uses a thermostat that switches around the setpoint (±0.3 K), with a minimum run of 15 min and a minimum pause of 10 min. It draws 1.0–3.0 kW of electricity (more the colder the apartment is). Efficiency (COP) = 2.6 + 0.09 × outdoor temperature, kept between 2.2 and 4.5.
- **Regular schedule**: 21 °C from 06:00 to 22:30, 18 °C at night. It runs the same way whether she is home or not.
- **Holiday mode** (16 °C, hot water off) only on the 3 of 10 trips where she remembers: Brussels, Łódź and Stockholm (7–9 Oct). That is 30 %.
- **Hot water**: the same heat pump heats a 150 L tank. It reheats at 1.6 kW (COP 2.5) when the tank has lost more than 2 kWh of heat. The tank loses 1.2 kWh of heat per day even when nobody is home. Heat drawn: shower 1.4–1.8 kWh (Paul 1.6–2.0), 0.05 kWh per hour when someone is awake at home, 0.3 kWh per hour extra with guests. A weekly anti-legionella heat-up runs on Sunday at 02:00.
- Annual service on 5 Oct: heat pump off 14:00–15:10, then a 20-minute test run at 2.4 kW.

# Forecast – assumptions

Scripts in `src/`, each run on its own (shared code in `src/hackowatt/`):
1. `generate_all.py` – weather, calendar, consumption, ground truth and geolocation data (see above)
2. `interpret_calendar.py` – the LLM reads each calendar day (cached in `data/llm_cache/<provider>_<model>/`)
3. `run_forecast.py <start> <hours>` – forecast, reminders and tips
4. `backtest.py` – forecasts over 29 Sep – 12 Oct vs actual
5. `export_app_data.py [now] [--out file]` – writes the forecast as JSON for the app (`data/app/forecast.json`, same keys as the app's demo data)

| Topic | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Presence (isHome) | Away score 0–100 per hour (0 = home for sure, 100 = away for sure). Priority: geolocation (position < 30 min old) → LLM calendar reading (only if ≤ 15 or ≥ 85) → habits. |
| LLM | Default Google Gemini `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` (key: `GEMINI_API_KEY` or `API_KEY` in git-ignored `.secret`); alternatives Ollama (local) and Claude via `ISHOME_PROVIDER`. One call per calendar day (day ± 1 day), cached; re-run only when that day's entries change. |
| LLM prompt | Only generic instructions + the home address + facts learned automatically from data up to 28 Sep (first kitchen use, last evening activity, nights away, usual weekday leave/return, frequent places from phone positions). No names or hand-written knowledge about her life or her calendar. |
| Habits | Share of time away per hour of day, weekday vs weekend, from past phone positions. |
| Weather | Open-Meteo forecasts as issued (Historical Forecast API), `data/weather/weather_forecast_hourly.csv`. The simulation uses the observed weather, so the forecast carries a realistic weather error. |
| Heat pump | Same default physics as the simulation (60 W/K, 8 MJ/K, COP curve). Tank assumed half full at forecast start. Band ±20 %. The *real* forecast assumes she forgets holiday mode (her usual behaviour), so trips where she remembers it are over-forecast. |
| Washer / dishwasher | Rules on the isHome output + last run; usual start hours from past runs. Ideal = cheapest allowed delay-start within 24 h (washer must finish while she is home, not within 12 h before a trip ≥ 1 night; among equal prices the latest slot). |
| Activities, baseload | Average per hour of day from the past; activities only from hours she was home, × (1 − away score / 100). |
| Real vs ideal | Ideal = 16 °C, tank off and standby −15 W while away score ≥ 85 (back to normal 2 h before return) + shifting (tank reheated 03–06 h, pre-heat 22 °C 15–17 h, 20 °C during 17–22 h) + washer/dishwasher in cheapest allowed slot. |
| Reminders & tips | Away-mode reminder 12 h before departure if saving over the whole absence ≥ €1 and away score ≥ 85. Washer/dishwasher tip 1 h before usual start if chance ≥ 60 % and saving ≥ €0.10. Heat pump setting tip once, saving scaled to 30 days. |
| Tariff | 00–06 €0.18 · 06–17 €0.28 · 17–22 €0.40 · 22–24 €0.28 (HackoWatt common assumptions). |
